# Week 1: Strategic Planning and Data Exploration in Logistics
**Logistics Data Science Internship Project**

---

## 1. Project Scenario & Objective
* **Scenario:** *Urban Last-Mile E-Commerce Delivery Optimization.* Addressing distribution network delays, rising fuel costs, and urban traffic congestion through data-driven planning.
* **Objective:** Simulate the planning and strategy phase of a logistics project, identify core Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), and define an end-to-end analytical roadmap using Python.

---

## 2. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
1. **On-Time Delivery Rate (OTDR):** Percentage of shipments successfully delivered within the promised time window (Target: >95%).
2. **Average Transit Time:** Mean duration from warehouse dispatch to customer doorstep delivery.
3. **Cost per Kilometer (CPK):** Total transportation and fuel expenditures divided by total distance traveled.

---

## 3. Data Science Methodologies
* **Predictive Regression:** To forecast exact delivery completion intervals based on distance, traffic, and package weight.
* **K-Means Clustering:** For spatial segmentation of delivery zones and warehouse micro-hub placement.
* **Optimization Algorithms:** To minimize route distance and fuel burn during peak congestion hours.

---

## 4. Strategic End-to-End Roadmap
1. **Data Ingestion:** Aggregating historical delivery logs, GPS telematics, and traffic indexes.
2. **Data Preprocessing:** Handling missing data pings and isolating outliers.
3. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Assessing descriptive statistics and correlation matrices.
4. **Predictive Modeling:** Implementing machine learning models (Random Forest) for timeline forecasting.
